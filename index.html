Common questions

I bought the Pro Detailer License but still see ads. Open the Shop tab (cart button in the top bar) and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Are ads required? No. Ads are always optional: watch one for a +50% cash bonus on a job or to unlock a VIP Disaster job. Normal progress never needs an ad.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the EU, UK and Switzerland, open the pause menu and tap Privacy choices.

The game feels slow or my phone gets warm. Open the pause menu and lower Graphics to Low or the frame rate to 60 Hz.
