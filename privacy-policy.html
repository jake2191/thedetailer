The Detailer: Upholstery Sim — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game The Detailer: Upholstery Sim (“the game”, “we”) uses, and why. The game is published by Jake McGuinness. If you have any questions, contact us at jake2191@gmail.com.

Short version

  • We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or microphone.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you buy the Pro Detailer License, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Your cash, jobs, unlocked equipment and settings (such as graphics quality) are saved locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads, for example to earn a cash bonus after a job. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are never required to progress in the game. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the identifier for vendors (IDFV);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped).

The game does not ask for permission to track you through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency, so it does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalise ads. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time from the game’s pause menu (“Privacy choices”). If you don’t consent, you may still be offered non-personalised ads, which use only limited data (for example, for frequency capping and fraud prevention).

We rely on your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Article 6(1)(f)) for non-personalised advertising.

4. In-app purchase (Pro Detailer License)

The optional Pro Detailer License is a one-time purchase made through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The game only receives confirmation from Apple that the license was purchased, so it can remove ads and apply the cash bonus. Owning the license stops all ad requests.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and purchase processing described above, we do not share information with third parties.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because the game stores your progress only on your device, deleting the game removes it. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can manage your choices in the game’s “Privacy choices” menu and at Google’s Ad Settings. You may also contact us at the address above, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
